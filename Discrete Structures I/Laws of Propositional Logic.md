- <u>Identity Laws: </u>
		- p ∧ T ≡ p
		- p ∨ F ≡ p
- <u>Domination Laws:</u>
	- p ∨ T ≡ T
	- p ∧ F ≡ F
- <u>Idempotent Laws:</u>
	- p ∨ p ≡ p
	- p ∧ p ≡ p
- <u>Double Negation:</u>
	- ¬(¬p) ≡ p
- <u>Complement Laws:</u>
	- p ∨ ¬p ≡ T
	- p ∧ ¬p ≡ F
- <u>Commutative Laws:</u>
	- p ∨ q ≡ q ∨ p
	- p ∧ q ≡ q ∧ p
- <u>Associative Laws:</u>
	- (p ∨ q) ∨ r ≡ p ∨ (q ∨ r)
	- (p ∧ q) ∧ r ≡ p ∧ (q ∧ r)
- <u>Distributive Laws:</u>
	- p ∨ (q ∧ r) ≡ (p ∨ q) ∧ (p ∨ r)
	- p ∧ (q ∨ r) ≡ (p ∧ q) ∨ (p ∧ r)
- <u>De Morgan’s Laws:</u>
	- ¬(p ∧ q) ≡ ¬p ∨ ¬q
	- ¬(p ∨ q) ≡ ¬p ∧ ¬q
- <u>Absorption Laws:</u>
	- p ∨ (p ∧ q) ≡ p
	- p ∧ (p ∨ q) ≡ p
- <u>Conditional Laws:</u>
	- p → q ≡ ¬p ∨ q
	- p ↔ q ≡ (p → q) ∧ (q → p)
